- **Linux video and audio playback in `rune-media`.** `Player` now decodes video (`.mp4`/`.mov` and other FFmpeg-supported containers) and audio (`.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, etc.) on Linux through dynamically linked system FFmpeg (`ffmpeg-next`) and `cpal` (PipeWire/PulseAudio with ALSA fallback). Linux builds now require FFmpeg, ALSA and libclang development headers at build time, while apps that do not construct a `Player` do not link FFmpeg or ALSA at runtime.
